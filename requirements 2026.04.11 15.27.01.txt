NumPy (numpy): 

​Used for generating the synthetic correlated signal distributions.

​Powers the core Linear Algebra engine (np.linalg.inv) to invert the covariance matrix.

​Handles the vector-matrix multiplication (@ operator) for signal aggregation.

​Pandas (pandas): 

​Used for structured data manipulation.

​While the current core logic is NumPy-based, Pandas is required for real-world "Quant" workflows where signals are indexed by timestamps and asset tickers.
